VU3INI PDF Swiss Knife is a free desktop application for Windows 10 and 11 that brings everyday and advanced PDF work into one place, without uploading anything to the cloud. It merges, splits and reorders pages and adds page numbers and Bates stamps. It edits and annotates, redacts permanently, and fills or builds forms. It converts PDFs to and from Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Markdown, images and web pages, and also compresses files, archives them as PDF/A and repairs damaged ones. For security it supports drawn or digital (PKI) signatures and AES-256 encryption. Scanned documents become searchable through OCR in 21 languages, including Assamese, Hindi and Bengali, and documents can be translated with their layout preserved. A built-in AI assistant answers questions about a PDF and links each answer to the page it came from. Everything is bundled in one installer and works without an internet connection; cloud translation and cloud AI are optional extras. Built with Python, Qt and MuPDF by Sasanka Boruah (VU3INI).
